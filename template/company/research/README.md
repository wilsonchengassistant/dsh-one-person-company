from "inferred", and there is an explicit "what we could not verify".

## How to read these

They are **research, not doctrine**. Two of them directly contradict widely
repeated claims — the METR "19% slower" figure (superseded by METR itself) and
the "36.3% of startups are one-person" statistic (no primary source). Where a
brief flags something as unverified, treat it as unverified.

The playbook in `playbook/` is where the claims were turned into decisions;
the briefs are where you can check the decisions.

## What a new brief needs

- A source URL and a date on every claim
- A section titled "what we could not verify"
- One recommendation the PM role can act on
- A note on whether the finding changes anything already in `playbook/`
